ABOUT PLATFORM

Civic Technology For Better Communities

CivicConnect is a civic-tech platform designed for Pakistani citizens to report local public issues such as garbage collection, electricity failures, gas shortages, water problems, and road damage.

The platform supports English, Roman Urdu, and Urdu complaint generation to make reporting easier for everyone.

🌍 Multi-language Support

English, Roman Urdu, and Urdu complaint input.

🏛 Department Mapping

Connect issues with the correct civic department.

⚡ Fast Complaint Generation

Generate structured complaint drafts instantly.
