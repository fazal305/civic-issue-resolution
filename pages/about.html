ABOUT CIVICCONNECT

Civic Technology For Better Communities

CivicConnect is a civic-tech platform designed to help Pakistani citizens report public issues such as garbage, electricity, water, gas, and road damage.

The platform supports English, Roman Urdu, and Urdu so more citizens can understand, prepare, and submit structured civic complaints easily.

This project is currently being developed as an Aptech Vision Project by Team Sufyan, Fazal, and Sana, with a focus on real-world civic problem solving, accessibility, and clean frontend engineering.

Report An Issue View Dashboard

🌍 Multilingual Reporting

Users can prepare complaints in English, Roman Urdu, and Urdu.

🏛 Civic Department Mapping

Issues can be connected with departments such as KMC, KWSC, K-Electric, and SSGC.

☁ Firebase Cloud Backend

Complaints are saved to Firebase Firestore for cloud-based tracking and future expansion.

📊 Complaint Dashboard

The dashboard supports complaint cards, search, filtering, pagination, and status indicators.
